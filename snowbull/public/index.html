Your investing assistant, with receipts

Ask about stocks, funds, bonds or your plan. SnowBull answers from a vetted library plus live market data, shows its sources, and flags scams and risky trades before they cost you.

Add your holdings for personalised checks (optional)

One per line: SYMBOL amount, e.g. AAPL 5000. Add cash 2000 for cash. Stays in your browser tab; it is sent only with your questions so SnowBull can check concentration and risk.

📚Sourced answers

Every answer is retrieved from a curated library (SEC, FINRA, IRS, FTC guidance) and cited, so you can verify it. If it has no reliable source, it says so.

⚡Current, not stale

Live quotes and headlines are retrieved at question time and ranked by relevance and freshness, so answers reflect today, not a training cut-off.

🧭Practical guidance

Asset-mix starting points, tradeoffs and next steps matched to your horizon and risk comfort, with your holdings if you share them.

🛡️Scam & risk alerts

A rules-based detector scans every message for fraud patterns (guaranteed returns, pump-and-dump, phishing) and trading risks (leverage, concentration, unusual moves).

Privacy & limits

Do not enter passwords, one-time codes, card numbers or seed phrases. Conversations are not stored on a server by this app. SnowBull is not a licensed adviser and cannot guarantee that information is complete or current.
